## Trademark / name check (Oct 22, 2026, quick web search; not legal advice)
- No USPTO filing found for "STITCH & SNUGGLE" or "STITCH AND SNUGGLE".
- Closest mark: **SNUGGLE STITCH**, USPTO serial 99754534, filed by Snuggle Stitch LLC on Apr 9, 2026, with a Notice of Allowance on Oct 6, 2026. It is in **Class 25 (maternity and infant clothing)**, not Class 9 or 41 (software and games). It uses the same two words reversed, so the risk is low to moderate.
- Small businesses using the name, unregistered as far as I found: Stitch & Snuggle Boutique LLC (baby knits, US), stitchandsnuggle.com (blankets, AU), Stitch n Snuggle (latch-hook kits, Lebanon), Snuggle & Stitch (UK baby wear), and a "Stitch & Snuggle Studios" shop on Ribblr.
- App Store: no app with this name was found. The nearest is "stitch." (an embroidery puzzle game by Lykkegaard).
- Also note: "Stitch" is Disney's Lilo & Stitch character. Our use is the plain sewing verb, but don't use any blue alien imagery.
- Recommendation: OK to proceed. Before submitting, do a proper USPTO search for Classes 9 and 41, and maybe file an intent-to-use application. Have Cuddle Clinic and Patch Pals ready as fallbacks.
